Add an account

The new account signs in with this password and an authenticator code; its key is shown once, after it is created.

  1. Open Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator and add an account.
  2. Scan the QR code, or choose "Enter a setup key" and type the key below (time-based).
  3. Sign in with the username, the password and the 6-digit code the app shows.

This key is shown only now. Give it to the account's owner over a private channel.
