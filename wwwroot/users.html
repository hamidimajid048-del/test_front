Roles

  • A role is a named set of permissions. Choosing it when adding or editing an account fills in its permissions; changing a role later does not change accounts that already used it. The Accounts column lists the accounts whose access is exactly this role; super admins hold every permission and are counted under Full access. Use Show permissions to see what a role allows on each dashboard.
