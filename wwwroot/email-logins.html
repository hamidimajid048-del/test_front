Email info

Logins

  • Every MetaTrader account with this email, read from the users table of the MetaTrader backup: active ones and archived ones, marked "Archived". At most 100 logins are reported together.
  • Devices and Computer IDs come from the login sessions in the date range: the platforms each login signed in with, and the computer IDs (CID) it used, the most used first, with the number of sessions.
  • The buttons open that login's own report in a new browser tab.

IP

CID

  • IP and CID: for each login, the IPs and computer IDs it signed in from within the date range, with the first and last sign-in and the number of sessions. Sorting, filters and paging apply to all rows.

Positions

  • The positions of every login of the email that were opened in the date range, at most a year, read from the MetaTrader backup; a position still open at the end of the range shows where it stands now.
