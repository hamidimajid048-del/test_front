Summary

  • Logins: the recorded logins of that login on the searched value, over the applied range.
  • Name, Group, Email, Balance: come with the report from the MetaTrader backup and can be sorted, filtered and exported like the other columns. Archived accounts are marked "(archived)"; "Not found" means the login is not in the backup yet.
  • Click a row to see that login's own sessions, or open the Details tab for every session of every login at once.
