• Watching: the market is open and ticks arrive. Gap open: no tick since the time shown; the stages of the active window fire as the gap grows. Closed: the symbol's quote session is closed, the server has a holiday, or trading is disabled; nothing is sent. Uncovered: no time window covers this moment. Snoozed: silenced from this page for a while.
  • All times are MetaTrader server time, the same clock as the symbol sessions and the holidays.

Profiles

A profile is one symbol with its time windows. Each window (server time) has stages: after how many seconds without a tick a channel is told, how many times and how far apart. Windows of a profile must not overlap; a moment outside every window is not monitored.

Channels

A channel is a Telegram chat the bot posts to. Telegram cannot force a loud notification: set the sound of the wake-up channel on your phone and keep it out of silent mode.

Monitoring

Tick monitoring When on, the engine watches every enabled profile and sends the Telegram alarms. It needs a saved bot token; without one it stays off.

Telegram bot

Create the bot with @BotFather in Telegram and paste the token it gives (it looks like 123456789:AAH…). The token is kept in the panel database and is never shown again; only its last characters are. Leave the field empty to keep the saved token.

Sends "Tick monitor test message" to the chosen channel with the saved token. If you pasted a new token, save first. The bot must be a member of the chat and allowed to post.

Operations channel

Receives only the service notices: MetaTrader connection lost or restored, monitoring started or stopped, and a stalled engine. Without a channel they are written to the log only. Symbol alarms never go here; they go to the channels chosen in each profile's stages. Channels are managed on the Channels tab.

Limits and timing

The three limits are checked when a profile is saved; lowering a limit does not change existing profiles until they are edited. The grace period and the history retention apply at once.
