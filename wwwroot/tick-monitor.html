• Watching: the market is open and ticks arrive. Gap open: no tick since the time shown; the stages of the active window fire as the gap grows. Closed: the symbol's quote session is closed, the server has a holiday, or trading is disabled; nothing is sent. Uncovered: no time window covers this moment. Snoozed: silenced from this page for a while.
  • All times are MetaTrader server time, the same clock as the symbol sessions and the holidays.

Profiles

A profile is one symbol with its time windows. Each window (server time) has stages: after how many seconds without a tick a channel is told, how many times and how far apart. Windows of a profile must not overlap; a moment outside every window is not monitored.

Channels

A channel is a Telegram chat the bot posts to. Telegram cannot force a loud notification: set the sound of the wake-up channel on your phone and keep it out of silent mode.

Settings

  • The bot token is kept in the panel database and never shown again; the field shows only its last characters. Leave it empty to keep the saved token.
  • The operations channel receives the service notices: MetaTrader connection lost or restored, monitoring started or stopped, and a stalled engine.
  • The limits apply when a profile is saved; lowering a limit does not change existing profiles until they are edited.
