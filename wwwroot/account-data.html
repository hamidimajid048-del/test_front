Balance growth

  • Start balance: read from the account comment: the whole comment when it is a number, otherwise the part after its last colon (Prop:...:...:StartBalance).
  • Net profit: profit, commission, swap and fee of all trading deals; deposits, withdrawals, credit and bonus are not included.
  • Growth chart: the balance after each deal that changed it, in money or as the percent change against the start balance.
