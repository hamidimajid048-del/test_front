  • Every account on the MetaTrader server, read from the Manager. The range is whole UTC days and can be at most 3 days long.
  • Positions: positions opened in the range, with their closing deals even when they closed after it. Orders: history orders of the range and the active orders placed in it. Deals: every deal of the range.
  • Sorting, column filters and paging apply to all rows. Numbers and dates also accept >, <, >=, <= and =. The CSV and Excel buttons download the rows of the page shown.
