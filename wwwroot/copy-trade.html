Search & Filters

  • Share count: the number of this login's positions matched with a position of the related login (each position is matched at most once).
  • Share (%): the share count divided by all positions of this login.
  • Name, Group, Email, Balance: read from MetaTrader after the table loads. Active accounts come from the Manager; archived accounts, marked "(archived)", from the Administrator archive.
  • Different email only hides the rows on this page whose email equals the searched login's email. Rows whose email is unknown stay visible.
  • Compare logins opens the sessions comparison of the two accounts (shared IPs and CIDs, near logins). Positions opens the position comparison below.
